# 5 element rougelite tower defense: towers
## Elements
### Example
 - Damage: The range of damage depending on other element
 - Speed: The range of fire speed depending on other element
 - Effect?: if the tower has an effect the effect (Effect impact)

Notes...
### Fire
 - Damage: Medium - High
 - Speed: Slow - Medium
 - Effect: Burning (Medium Effect)

Fire is the first element you get and is a pretty all around neutral element with all stats being around medium and burn being a medium impact effect
### Ice
 - Damage: Low - Medium
 - Speed: Medium - Fast
 - Effect: Slow (Sub-Major Effect)

Ice is the second element and foucses more on slowing and faster hit speed

### Earth
 - Damage: Medium - High
 - Speed: Medium - High

Earth is the third/fourth element and is foucsed on being a big tanky element dealing more damage

### Air
 - Damage: Low - Medium
 - Speed: Fast - Medium

Air is the third/fourth element and is foucsed on being a fast and light attacker

### Electricity
 - Damage: Low - High
 - Speed: Slow - Fast
 - Effect: Stun

Electricity is the last element and mostly depends on what it is combined with to get its stats
